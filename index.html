Beginner Guide to Swimming



Swimming Rules
  • Stay in your assigned lane.
  • Start on the signal, wait before diving.
  • Follow the correct swimming technique.
  • Touch the wall at the finish.
  • Do not interfere with other swimmers during a competition.
Swimming Equipment
  • Goggles
  • Head Cap
  • Fins
  • Kickboard
  • Pull Buoy
  • Paddles
Basic Techniques
  • Butterfly
  • Backstroke
  • Breaststroke
  • Freestyle
